Our Story

Why can't every Indian home have internet as affordable as a cup of chai?

90% of Indian homes don't have Wi-Fi. Not because the technology doesn't exist — but because no one made it affordable enough. Wiom did. We turned home broadband into a simple recharge, starting at just a few rupees a day. Today, we're India's largest PM-WANI platform, backed by Accel, Prosus, BII, and RTP Global — on a mission to connect 50 crore lives by 2030.

Our Mission

Impact 50 crore lives by 2030.

80 crore Indians own smartphones. Only 10 crore homes have Wi-Fi. The gap isn't technology — it's distribution.

Wiom fixes this through PM-WANI — the government's open framework that turns any broadband connection into a public hotspot. We bring the platform. Local entrepreneurs bring the last mile.

Affordable internet, delivered by people who live in the communities they serve.

Our Journey

From a question to a movement

2015

Founded as i2e1

Satyam, Manaas, Nishit, and Ashutosh start building a platform to democratise internet access in India.

2020

PM-WANI Launched

Government of India launches PM-WANI. Company becomes one of India's first licensed PDOAs, rebrands to Wiom.

2023

Series A

Raised Series A from RTP Global, YourNest, and Omidyar Network India. Expanded to Delhi NCR, Mumbai, and Meerut.

2025

Series B

Raised Series B led by Accel and BII, with Prosus, RTP Global, and Promaft Partners.

Today & Beyond

Scaling Across India

Deepening penetration into non-metro India. Building the team. Onboarding operators. Connecting the next 50 crore.

Our Values

The best idea wins — regardless of who said it. We're one team solving one massive problem together.

50 crore lives. That's what matters. Every decision, every trade-off, every sprint — the mission comes first.

User Centricity

How we solve problems

1

Our users aren't personas on a slide. They're the Verma family in a tier-3 town. We feel the problem before we solve it.

No copy-paste solutions. We understand the "why" deeply, then build the simplest thing that works — and iterate fast.

Done beats perfect. Ship it, learn from it, improve it. 50 crore people can't wait for the perfect plan.

We're Always Learning & Unlearning

What worked yesterday might not work tomorrow. We stay curious, challenge assumptions, and grow with the mission.
