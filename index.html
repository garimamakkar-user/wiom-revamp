The Customer

For crores of Indian households who don't have home internet, Wiom is the most affordable solution. A family that streams, studies, and works from home — all on a flexible recharge that fits their monthly budget. No 3-month lock-ins. No hidden costs.

The Local Operator

For lakhs of small cable operators and local ISPs who fear competition from big telecom players, Wiom is a lifeboat. They already have the wires and cables. Wiom gives them a platform to double their revenue without extra infrastructure investment — turning an existential threat into a growth opportunity.

For the technicians who work under operators and have big dreams, Wiom is a mentor. They install, maintain, and troubleshoot connections on the ground. Wiom gives them dedicated tech tools, training, and a share of success — turning them from wire-pullers into empowered service partners.

The technology layer that stitches it all together. Wiom provides the patented platform, billing engine, compliance framework, and customer experience — connecting ISPs, operators, technicians, and households into one seamless ecosystem. Zero infrastructure owned. 50 crore lives to impact.
