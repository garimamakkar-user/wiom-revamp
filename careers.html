A Note from Our Founder
S

Satyam Darmora

Founder & CEO, Wiom

When we started Wiom, people asked us: "Why would anyone build internet infrastructure for sachet-sized packs?"

Because 50 crore Indians can't afford anything more. And they shouldn't have to choose between data and dinner.

We're not a typical startup. We don't sit in air-conditioned offices debating features. Our team is in the field — onboarding operators in tier-3 towns, debugging connectivity in places most companies haven't heard of, and building products for users who've never had reliable broadband before.

If you want to work on problems that are real, urgent, and affect hundreds of millions of lives — you'll love it here. If you need a playbook for everything and prefer comfortable certainty — this probably isn't the right place.

We're building something that matters. Come build it with us.
